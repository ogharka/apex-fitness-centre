How to buy supplements online in Nepal safely

Buying online is convenient, but you cannot touch the tub first. These checks protect you.

Before you pay

  • Check that the seller has a real address, phone number and reviews.
  • Ask for photos or a short video of the exact product.
  • Ask if you will get a bill.
  • Ask how long delivery takes and how it is sent.
  • Check the price against other sellers. Be careful if it is far lower.

How ordering works at Apex

When your parcel arrives

Check that the outer seal and the inner seal are unbroken and the batch number is printed. If anything looks wrong, take photos and message us the same day.

Returns

Sealed, unopened items can be returned. Opened supplements cannot be returned, so check everything before you open the tub.

Questions

Is it safe to pay in advance?

Pay only a seller you can verify. Check reviews, ask for photos, and keep the payment screenshot and bill.

Yes, by Khalti or bank transfer. We send details on WhatsApp.
