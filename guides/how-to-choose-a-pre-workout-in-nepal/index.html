How to choose a pre-workout in Nepal

A pre-workout is meant to help your energy and focus before training. It is optional, and the most important thing to check is the caffeine.

Check the caffeine first

Look for caffeine per serving on the label. Many people feel fine around 150 to 300 mg. If you are new or sensitive, start with half a serving. Do not combine it with coffee, energy drinks or other caffeine products.

Timing

Take it about 20 to 30 minutes before training, and avoid it late in the evening so it does not ruin your sleep.

Who should avoid it

People who are under 18, pregnant, sensitive to caffeine, or who have heart, blood pressure or anxiety problems should ask a doctor first. This is general information, not medical advice.

Questions

How much caffeine is in a pre-workout?

It varies. Check the label for caffeine per serving and start with a smaller amount if you are new.

Can I take pre-workout every day?

Many people take it only on harder training days to avoid building up tolerance and to protect their sleep.

Do I need a pre-workout?

No. It is optional. Sleep, food and a good plan matter more.
