What changes the price of creatine

Three things: the brand, the pack size and the seller. A bigger tub almost always costs less per gram. A very low price for a well-known brand is a warning sign, because it can mean an old, repacked or fake product.

Compare price per gram, not just the tub price. A 320 g tub at Rs. 2,000 is about Rs. 6.25 per gram. Divide the price by the grams in the pack and you can compare any two tubs.

Which type should you buy

Plain creatine monohydrate is the form with the most research behind it, and it is usually the cheapest. Flavoured tubs taste better but are not stronger. Unflavoured tubs mix into water or a shake without changing the taste.

You do not need a special form to get results. Choose a brand you trust, with a sealed tub and a printed batch number.

How much do you need

Most people take about 3 to 5 g a day, every day, including rest days. It builds up in your muscles over a few weeks, so taking it regularly matters more than the time of day. Follow the label on your tub, and check with a doctor first if you have a kidney or other health condition.

Questions

Is creatine safe?

Creatine monohydrate is widely studied and is considered safe for healthy adults at normal doses. People with kidney problems or other conditions should ask a doctor first. This is general information, not medical advice.

Do I need to load creatine?

No. A daily dose of about 3 to 5 g reaches the same level in your muscles after a few weeks. Loading only gets you there faster.
