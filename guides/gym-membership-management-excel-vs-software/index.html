Excel or notebook vs gym software

Many gyms start with a notebook or an Excel sheet. It works while you have few members. Here is where it starts to cost you money, and when software is worth it.

Where notebooks and Excel work

They are free and familiar. For a very small gym with a few dozen members, they can be enough.

Where they fail

  • Nobody tells you who is expiring this week.
  • Dues are easy to forget and hard to total.
  • Freezing a sick member means editing dates by hand.
  • Anyone can change a number and you cannot see who did it.
  • One lost file or one broken computer can erase everything.
  • Sending reminders means copying names and numbers one by one.
