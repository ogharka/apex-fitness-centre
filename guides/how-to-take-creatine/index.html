How to take creatine

Creatine is simple to use, but there is a lot of confusing advice. Here is the plain version.

The simple way

  • Take about 3 to 5 g of creatine monohydrate a day. Check the scoop size on your tub.
  • Take it every day, including rest days.
  • Any time works. Pick a time you will remember, such as with your post-workout shake or with breakfast.
  • Mix it in water, juice or a shake, and drink water through the day.

Do you need to load?

No. Loading means taking about 20 g a day for 5 to 7 days, then dropping to a normal dose. It fills your muscles faster, but a normal daily dose reaches the same level after a few weeks.

What to expect

Creatine is not a steroid and does not give instant results. Over weeks it can help you do a little more work in the gym, such as an extra rep. Results vary from person to person, and training, food and sleep matter much more.

Who should be careful

Ask a doctor first if you have a kidney condition, take regular medicines, or are under 18. This is general information, not medical advice.

Questions

Should I take creatine on rest days?

Yes. Take it every day so the level in your muscles stays up.

Does creatine make you gain weight?

It can cause a small rise in body weight in the first weeks because muscles hold more water. This is normal and not fat gain.

Which creatine should I buy in Nepal?

Choose creatine monohydrate from a brand you trust, in a sealed tub with a batch number, from a seller who gives a bill.
