Gym management software price in Nepal

If you own a gym in Nepal, you will see two kinds of pricing for gym software: a monthly subscription, or one payment. This guide explains the difference, what to check before you buy, and what a one-time price looks like.

Monthly plans versus one-time payment

Many gym apps charge a fee every month, often based on the number of members. That feels cheap at first, but over two or three years the total is often higher than a one-time price, and you stop having the software when you stop paying.

A one-time payment costs more on day one, but you pay once and keep using it. For a gym that runs for many years, one payment is usually the cheaper choice.

What to check before you buy any gym software

  • Does it show who is expiring this week, with days left?
  • Does it separate membership dues from product dues?
  • Can you freeze a member who is sick or travelling?
  • Does it back up your data automatically?
  • Can you limit what each staff member can do?
  • Does it work if the internet is down?
  • Who will help you set it up and train staff?
