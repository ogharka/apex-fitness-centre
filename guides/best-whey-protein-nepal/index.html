Do you even need whey?

Whey is simply milk protein in powder form. It is convenient, not magic. You need it only if you struggle to reach your daily protein target from food. Work out your number with our guide on how much protein you need.

Many popular tubs are blends of concentrate and isolate, with extras like digestive enzymes, creatine or MCT. Read the label so you know what you are paying for.

5 things to check before you buy

  1. Protein per scoop. A good whey gives roughly 20 to 25 g of protein per scoop and around 70% protein or more by weight. A lot of carbohydrate and sugar means it is closer to a mass gainer.
  2. Price per kg, not per tub. A cheaper 1 kg tub is often dearer per kg than a 2 kg tub (see the table above).
  3. A genuine product. Check the seal, batch number, expiry date, importer label and authenticity code. Our guide on how to spot fake supplements in Nepal explains each check.
  4. Extras you may not need. If your protein already has creatine, you may not need a separate creatine tub.
  5. Taste and mixability. You will drink it every day. Chocolate and vanilla are safe choices.

How to compare protein prices

Do not look only at the tub price. Divide the price by the kilograms in the pack to get the price per kg, then divide it by the number of servings to get the price per shake. Next, check the label for protein per serving: a cheaper tub can give less protein per scoop.

How to use whey

  • One scoop a day is enough for most people; two if your protein from food is very low.
  • Timing does not matter much. After training or between meals both work; your total protein for the day matters most.
  • Mix with water (lighter) or milk (more calories, good for weight gain).
  • Whey does not replace meals. Keep eating dal bhat, eggs and the rest.

How much protein do you need

Many active adults aim for about 1.4 to 2.0 g of protein per kg of body weight a day, from food and shakes together. A shake adds to your meals and does not replace them. If you eat enough protein from dal, eggs, paneer, soya and meat, you may need less powder.

Is whey protein safe?

For healthy adults at normal amounts, yes. It is a food made from milk. If you have kidney disease or a milk allergy, ask your doctor first.

Can I take whey without going to the gym?

You can, but you probably do not need it. Whey helps most when you train and struggle to eat enough protein.

Whey or mass gainer: which is better?

Whey is mainly protein. Mass gainers add lots of carbohydrate, often sugar, for extra calories. For weight gain, milk with banana, oats and peanuts plus a scoop of whey is often cheaper and cleaner.

Does whey cause hair loss or acne?

There is no good evidence that whey causes hair loss. Some people notice acne with dairy products; if that happens, try less or switch to an isolate.
