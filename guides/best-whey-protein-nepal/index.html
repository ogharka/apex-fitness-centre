How to compare protein prices

Do not look only at the tub price. Divide the price by the kilograms in the pack to get the price per kg, then divide it by the number of servings to get the price per shake. Next, check the label for protein per serving: a cheaper tub can give less protein per scoop.

How much protein do you need

Many active adults aim for about 1.4 to 2.0 g of protein per kg of body weight a day, from food and shakes together. A shake adds to your meals and does not replace them. If you eat enough protein from dal, eggs, paneer, soya and meat, you may need less powder.
