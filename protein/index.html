Whey is a quick-mixing protein that many lifters use after training or between meals. It does not replace food, it adds to it.

Why add whey to your day

How to use whey

  1. After training. One serving in water or milk within a couple of hours of your workout.
  2. Between meals. A shake is a quick way to add protein on busy days.
  3. Mix it into food. Stir into oats, curd or a smoothie for a high-protein snack.
  4. Keep food first. Whey adds to a balanced diet. It does not replace meals.
