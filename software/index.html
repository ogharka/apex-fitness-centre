What Apex Gym Management does

Simple enough for a front desk on a busy morning. Complete enough for the owner at night.

Members and renewals

Add a member in under a minute. Renew in two clicks. Every member shows a clear status: Active, Expiring soon with days left, Expired or Frozen.

Freeze and unfreeze

Sick or travelling? Freeze the membership with a note. The days stop, and are added back when they return. No more arguments about lost days.

Dues you can actually collect

Membership dues and product dues are shown separately and collected on one screen. Everything, membership only or products only. Receipts are made for you.

WhatsApp reminders

Due, expiring and expired reminders with the real name and amount filled in. English and Nepali templates. Send to one person or a whole list.

Sales and Quick Tab

Sell drinks and supplements, track stock, and keep a credit tab for regular members. Stock goes down on sale and back on cancel.

Payments and receipts

Search every payment by name, ID, phone or receipt number. Filter by date and mode: cash, bank or eSewa. Totals update as you filter. Print receipts as PDF.

Attendance

Check members in by name, ID or phone. Each member is counted once per day. See any past day.

Staff, clock-in and tasks

Staff check in and out, follow a daily checklist and read your messages. You see who has seen them.

Expenses and reports

Record rent, salary and bills. See income, expenses, profit and dues for any date range.

Daily auto backup

A backup is saved every morning by itself, with an optional second copy on a USB drive or Drive folder.

Staff permissions and history

Every user has a login. You choose what each person can do. Cancelled and corrected records are never lost; they stay with the reason.

Customer bills in PDF

Make a clean bill with your gym name, address and PAN and download it as PDF.

Questions

Before you decide

How much does gym management software cost in Nepal?

Apex Gym Management costs Rs. 25,000 as a one-time payment. There is no monthly fee and no per-member fee. Many online gym systems charge every month, so a one-time price usually works out cheaper within a year or two.

What is included in the Rs. 25,000?

The full program with members, renewals, freeze, dues, WhatsApp reminders, sales, attendance, staff, expenses, reports, bills and automatic daily backup. We also install it, add your plans and prices, and train your staff.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay Rs. 25,000 once. You do not pay again each month or each year to keep using the program you bought.

What is Apex Gym Management?

It is the same software we use every day to run Apex Fitness Centre in Urlabari, Morang. It handles members, renewals, attendance, payments, dues, product sales, expenses, bills, staff and reports in one program.

Do I need internet to use it?

No. It runs on your gym computer and keeps your data on that computer. You only need internet to open WhatsApp links when you send reminders.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows and Mac. Most gyms use one Windows computer at the front desk.

Can it send WhatsApp reminders to members?

It prepares the message with the member's name and the exact amount due and opens WhatsApp with the text ready. You press Send. WhatsApp does not allow any software to send messages fully automatically, so we do not pretend it can.

How does the gym membership freeze feature work?

Press Freeze on a member, write a short note, and the days stop counting from that day. When the member returns, press Unfreeze and the paused days are added back to their end date.

What happens to my data? Is there a backup?

The program saves a backup by itself every morning when the computer is on. You can also pick a second folder, for example a USB drive or a Google Drive folder, so a copy is always somewhere else.

Can I control what my staff can do?

Yes. Every staff member has their own login and you choose which buttons they can use. For example, only the owner can cancel a payment. Every correction and cancellation is kept in a history with the reason.

Is it better than Excel or a notebook for a gym?

Excel and notebooks cannot tell you who is expiring this week, who owes money, or who changed a receipt. This software does that automatically, backs itself up, and reminds members on WhatsApp.

Can you set it up for my gym?
