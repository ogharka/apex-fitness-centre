Author

Suman Limbu

Owner and trainer, Apex Fitness Centre

Suman runs Apex Fitness Centre in Urlabari, Morang. Before opening the gym he played football, and he kept training through his years working abroad. Training has been part of his life ever since, and he keeps studying fitness and nutrition research. In more than 2 years of coaching at the gym he has helped over 100 members, especially beginners, people losing weight and women starting strength training. His motto: “Fitness is a journey.”

Suman writes or checks every article on this blog against the sources linked in it, and updates articles when the facts change. How we work: editorial policy. Where he trains members: Apex Fitness Centre, Urlabari.
