Choosing creatine

Creatine is stored in your muscles and helps supply quick energy for short, hard efforts like heavy sets and sprints. It is one of the most studied sports supplements.

Which one should you pick? Choose a flavoured tub (MuscleBlaze Citrus Blast or Wellcore Fruit Fusion) if you like a taste in your drink. Choose the unflavoured BPI Sports tub if you want to mix it into anything without changing the flavour.

What creatine does for you

More strength and power

Creatine helps your muscles make quick energy for heavy sets and sprints, so you can often squeeze out extra reps over time.

Better lean mass over time

Used with training and enough protein, creatine is linked with greater lean muscle gains.

Simple and cheap

One small serving a day. No cycling needed and no timing tricks. Consistency is what counts.

How to take creatine

  1. Take a small serving every day. Studies commonly use 3 to 5 g a day. Follow the label.
  2. Any time works. Pick a time you will remember, like with your post-workout shake.
  3. Stay consistent. Take it on rest days too. Creatine builds up in your muscles over a few weeks.
  4. Drink water through the day. Check with a doctor first if you have a kidney or other medical condition.
