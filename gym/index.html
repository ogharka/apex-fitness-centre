          Questions

          Before you join

          What do I pay when I join?

          Your first payment is the membership price plus Rs. 500 admission and Rs. 500 for your Apex T-shirt and access card. For example, 1 month is Rs. 2,500: Rs. 1,500 membership and Rs. 1,000 one-time. The 12-month plan has a lower joining cost: Rs. 12,500 in total for new members.

          Do I pay admission again when I renew?

          No. Renewals are the membership price only: Rs. 1,500 for 1 month, Rs. 4,000 for 3 months, Rs. 7,000 for 6 months and Rs. 12,000 for 12 months.

          What are your timings?

          Open Sunday to Friday from 4:30 AM to 8:30 PM, closed on Saturday. Suman Limbu is on duty in the morning, 4:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and Sanju Phombo Limbu in the evening, 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM.

          Can I see the gym before I join?

          Yes. Come in during opening hours, or send us a WhatsApp message and we will tell you the best time to visit.

          Do you deliver supplements?

          Yes, we deliver all over Nepal. Tap Order on any product and send the message on WhatsApp. We confirm the price and delivery details with you before sending.

          Where are you?

          Urlabari-9, Durgapuri, Morang. Use the Open in Maps button in the Contact section.
