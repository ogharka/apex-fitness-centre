How to measure

Use a soft tape measure, keep it snug but not tight, and measure in the morning before eating. Take each measurement twice and use the average.

  • Neck: just below the Adam's apple, with the tape sloping slightly down at the front.
  • Waist (men): horizontally at the level of the belly button, relaxed, after breathing out.
  • Waist (women): at the narrowest point of the waist.
  • Hips (women): around the widest part of the buttocks.

How the calculator works

The US Navy method estimates body fat from body circumferences and height. It was developed by Hodgdon and Beckett for the US Navy and is still used by the US military for body composition checks. Like all field methods, it is an estimate: it can be several percentage points away from lab methods such as DEXA, especially for very lean or very muscular people.

Use it to track progress

The real value is in the trend. Measure once a month in the same way. If your body fat estimate and waist are going down while your strength goes up, you are losing fat and keeping muscle.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the US Navy body fat method?

It is usually within a few percentage points of lab methods for most people, but can be less accurate for very lean, very muscular or very large bodies. It is best for tracking change over time.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

For many adults, roughly 14 to 24% for men and 21 to 31% for women falls in the fitness to average ranges. What is healthy also depends on age, waist size and other health markers.

Why does the calculator need my neck size?

Neck size helps the formula separate body size from fat stored around the waist. A thicker neck usually reflects more muscle and a larger frame.

Is BMI or body fat better?

Body fat percentage tells you more about body composition, because BMI cannot tell muscle from fat. Using both, plus waist size, gives a fuller picture.

How often should I measure body fat?

Once every 4 weeks is enough. Body fat changes slowly, and daily or weekly readings mostly show measurement noise.
