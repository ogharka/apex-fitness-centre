Snacks are where many diets quietly fail: biscuits with tea, samosa from the corner shop, a packet of noodles at 4 PM. The fix is not to stop snacking but to **snack smarter**. Here are 20 healthy, cheap snacks you can find or make anywhere in Nepal.

*Protein amounts are rough estimates.*

## High-protein snacks (best for most goals)

1. **Boiled eggs** (2): about 12 g protein. Add salt, pepper or timur.
2. **Chana chatpate** with boiled chana, onion, tomato, lemon: about 10 to 12 g per bowl.
3. **Soybean sadeko** (soaked, boiled soy chunks with onion, chilli, lemon): about 20 to 25 g per serving.
4. **Curd** (a bowl): about 7 g. Add fruit or a spoon of honey.
5. **Paneer cubes** with salt and chilli: about 10 g per 50 g.
6. **Glass of milk**: about 8 g.
7. **Kwati** (sprouted mixed beans), boiled or as soup: about 10 g per bowl.
8. **Egg bhurji wrap** in a roti: about 15 g.
9. **Hung curd dip** with cucumber sticks: about 8 to 10 g.
10. **Whey shake** if you use protein powder: about 20 to 25 g per scoop.

## Healthy carbs and fruit

11. **Banana**: quick energy before training.
12. **Seasonal fruit**: guava, apple, orange, papaya, mango (watch portions in mango season).
13. **Boiled sweet potato (sakarkhand)**: filling and good before workouts.
14. **Roasted corn (makai)**: fibre and slow energy.
15. **Chiura with curd**: a classic quick meal.

## Nuts and seeds (portion control!)

16. **Peanuts**: a small handful (about 30 g) has about 7 g protein but plenty of calories.
17. **Roasted soybeans (bhatmas)**: crunchy and high in protein.
18. **Peanut butter on roti or toast**: 1 tablespoon, not half the jar.
19. **Mixed nuts and raisins**: good for weight gain, small amounts for weight loss.
20. **Pumpkin or sunflower seeds**: sprinkle on curd or salads.

## Best snacks by goal

| Goal | Choose | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Fat loss** | Eggs, soybean sadeko, curd, chana, fruit, cucumber | Nuts, peanut butter, chiura portions |
| **Muscle gain** | Milk, peanuts, banana shake, egg rolls, chiura with curd | Junk food "bulking" |
| **Before training** | Banana, chiura with curd, toast with peanut butter | Heavy, oily food |
| **After training** | Milk, eggs, curd, soybean, whey | Skipping food altogether |

## Snacks to cut down on

- **Samosa, pakoda, sel roti fried in lots of oil:** high in calories, low in protein.
- **Instant noodles:** mostly refined carbs, oil and salt.
- **Biscuits and namkeen:** easy to eat a whole packet without feeling full.
- **Sweet milk tea several times a day:** the sugar adds up.

You don't have to ban them. Just make them occasional, not daily.

## Make snacking easy

- **Boil 6 eggs** at the start of the day.
- **Soak and boil soybean or chana** for 2 to 3 days at a time.
- **Keep fruit visible** on the table.
- **Carry a snack** to work or college so you're not tempted by the shop.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the healthiest Nepali snack?

Boiled eggs, chana chatpate (with little oil) and soybean sadeko are among the best: high in protein, cheap and filling.

### Is chiura healthy?

Chiura (beaten rice) is a good source of energy. It's healthy in sensible portions, especially with curd or as part of a balanced meal.

### Are peanuts good for weight loss?

They are nutritious but high in calories. A small handful is fine; a whole packet is not.

### What should I eat in the evening before the gym?

A light snack 60 to 90 minutes before: banana, curd with fruit, or chiura with milk.
