Among common vegetarian foods in Nepal, **soybeans have the most protein per 100 g (about 18 g cooked), and tofu gives the most protein for its calories.** Chana and dal are good too, but they come with more carbohydrate, so you need more of them to get the same protein.

All numbers below are USDA values, as shown on our [food calorie chart](../../food/).

## Protein per 100 g and per 100 calories

| Food (cooked) | Protein per 100 g | Calories per 100 g | Protein per 100 kcal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Soybeans](../../food/soybeans-calories/) | 18.1 g | 171 kcal | 10.6 g |
| [Tofu](../../food/tofu-calories/) | 7.2 g | 61 kcal | 11.8 g |
| [Urad dal (boiled)](../../food/urad-dal-calories/) | 7.5 g | 105 kcal | 7.2 g |
| [Dal (lentils, with oil)](../../food/dal-calories/) | 8.4 g | 165 kcal | 5.1 g |
| [Rajma (with oil)](../../food/rajma-calories/) | 8.0 g | 180 kcal | 4.4 g |
| [Chana (with oil)](../../food/chana-calories/) | 8.1 g | 218 kcal | 3.7 g |

For comparison, two boiled eggs give about 12.5 g of protein for 142 kcal, or 8.8 g per 100 kcal ([egg calories](../../food/egg-calories/)).

## What the numbers mean

- **Soybeans win per bite.** Half a cup of cooked soybeans (90 g) gives about 16 g of protein. Bhatmas and soybean chunks are cheap and easy to find in Nepal.
- **Tofu wins per calorie.** It is light, so it fits well when you are losing weight. A 120 g piece gives about 8.6 g of protein for only 73 kcal.
- **Chana, rajma and dal are carb-rich protein foods.** They are excellent for fibre and energy, and their protein adds up across a day, but a bowl of chana is mostly carbohydrate.

## Per serving, the way you actually eat it

| Serving | Protein | Calories |
|---|---|---|
| Half cup cooked soybeans (90 g) | about 16.3 g | about 154 kcal |
| 1 piece tofu (120 g) | about 8.6 g | about 73 kcal |
| 1 katori dal (150 g) | about 12.6 g | about 248 kcal |
| 1 katori chana (150 g) | about 12.2 g | about 327 kcal |
| 1 katori rajma (150 g) | about 12.0 g | about 270 kcal |

## Easy ways to eat more of the best ones

- **Bhatmas sadeko:** roasted soybeans with onion, chilli and lemon, a classic Nepali snack.
- **Soybean chunk curry:** cook like a meat curry; soak the chunks first.
- **Tofu tarkari:** cube it and add to any vegetable curry for the last 5 minutes.
- **Thick dal:** cook your dal thicker and eat a full katori, not a few spoons.
- **Chana chaat:** chana with cucumber, onion, tomato and lemon for a filling snack.

## How much protein do vegetarians need?

The same as everyone else who trains: about 1.4 to 2.0 g per kg of body weight a day ([ISSN](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28642676/)). Mixing different plant proteins through the day, such as dal with rice, or soybeans with roti, gives your body all the building blocks it needs. Check your number with the [protein calculator](../../calculators/protein-calculator/).

## A high-protein vegetarian day

Here is how the foods above can add up across one day:

| Meal | Food | Protein (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | 2 boiled eggs, or 1 katori curd with fruit | 5 to 12.5 g |
| Khana | Rice, 1 katori thick dal, tofu tarkari (120 g tofu), saag | about 33 g |
| Snack | Bhatmas sadeko (half cup soybeans) | about 16 g |
| Dinner | 2 roti, 1 katori rajma or chana, salad | about 21 g |

That is roughly 75 to 80 g of protein without meat, and more if you add milk, curd or paneer. A 60 kg person who trains needs about 84 to 120 g, so add one or two more protein foods on training days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which vegetarian food has the most protein?
Among common foods in Nepal, cooked soybeans have the most protein per 100 g, about 18 g.

### Is tofu or soybean better for protein?
Soybeans have more protein per 100 g; tofu has more protein per calorie. For weight loss, tofu is lighter; for bulking, soybeans give more in one serving.

### How much protein is in chana?
About 8.1 g per 100 g cooked with oil, or about 12 g in a katori (150 g).

### Is dal enough protein for the gym?
Dal helps a lot, but most people who train need other protein foods too, such as soybeans, tofu, eggs, curd or paneer.

### Can vegetarians build muscle?
Yes. Eat enough total protein from a mix of foods and train with progressive weights.
