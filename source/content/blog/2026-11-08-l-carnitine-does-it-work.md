L-carnitine is often sold as a "fat burner," but **its effect on fat loss is small at best**. Reviews of the research suggest that, in some groups, it may add **a little extra weight loss over several months**, but nothing close to what a calorie deficit, protein and regular exercise achieve. If you buy it, think of it as an optional extra, not the plan.

## What is L-carnitine?

L-carnitine is a compound made in your body from amino acids and also found in **meat, fish and dairy**. Its main job is to help carry fatty acids into your cells' mitochondria, where they can be used for energy.

That job is why it's marketed for fat loss: the idea is "more carnitine, more fat burned." But for most healthy people, the body already has enough carnitine for this process. Adding more doesn't automatically burn more fat.

## What does the research show?

- **Weight loss:** pooled studies suggest a **modest** extra weight loss compared with placebo in some adults, often with overweight or obesity, usually alongside diet changes. The effect is small.
- **Exercise performance:** results are mixed. Some studies show small benefits for recovery or endurance; many show none.
- **Muscle soreness:** a few studies show slightly less soreness after hard exercise.

The people most likely to benefit are those with **low carnitine levels**, for example some vegetarians, older adults or people with certain medical conditions.

## Who might consider it

- You already have a good diet, train regularly, and want to try an extra.
- You are vegetarian and eat little dairy.
- You have tried it before and personally like it.

## Safety

At typical label doses, L-carnitine is generally well tolerated by healthy adults. Possible side effects include **stomach upset, nausea** or a **"fishy" body odour** at high doses. People with **thyroid conditions, kidney disease or a history of seizures**, and pregnant or breastfeeding women, should ask a doctor first.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does L-carnitine burn belly fat?

No supplement burns fat from a specific area. L-carnitine's overall fat-loss effect is small.

### Is liquid L-carnitine better than capsules?

There's no strong evidence that one form works better for fat loss. Choose the form you find convenient.

### Can women take L-carnitine?

Healthy adult women can generally use it at label doses. Pregnant or breastfeeding women should ask a doctor.

### How long does it take to work?

Studies that show an effect usually run for several months. Don't expect quick changes.
