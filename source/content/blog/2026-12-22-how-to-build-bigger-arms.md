Want bigger arms? Train **triceps as much as biceps**, because triceps make up roughly **two-thirds of your upper arm**. Combine heavy compound lifts (presses and rows) with direct arm work **2 times a week**, use full range of motion, and eat enough to grow.

## Arm anatomy in 30 seconds

- **Biceps:** front of the arm, bend the elbow and turn the palm up.
- **Triceps:** back of the arm, straighten the elbow. Three heads, including the long head that also crosses the shoulder.
- **Brachialis and brachioradialis:** under and beside the biceps, trained well by hammer curls. They add thickness.

## Best biceps exercises

| Exercise | Why |
|---|---|
| **Barbell or EZ-bar curl** | Lets you use more weight |
| **Incline dumbbell curl** | Stretches the biceps at the bottom |
| **Hammer curl** | Builds brachialis and forearm thickness |
| **Cable curl** | Constant tension through the whole movement |

## Best triceps exercises

| Exercise | Why |
|---|---|
| **Close-grip bench press** | Heavy compound triceps work |
| **Overhead dumbbell or cable extension** | Stretches the long head |
| **Cable pushdown** (rope or bar) | Easy to learn, great pump |
| **Dips** (or bench dips for beginners) | Bodyweight strength |

## A twice-a-week arm plan

Add this to the end of your upper body sessions:

**Day A**

- EZ-bar curl: 3 x 8–10
- Overhead triceps extension: 3 x 10–12
- Hammer curl: 2 x 10–12
- Rope pushdown: 2 x 12–15

**Day B**

- Close-grip bench press: 3 x 6–8
- Incline dumbbell curl: 3 x 10
- Dips or bench dips: 2 x as many good reps as possible
- Cable curl: 2 x 12–15

That's roughly **10 sets per week for each of biceps and triceps**, plus indirect work from presses and rows. See [how many sets and reps to build muscle](../how-many-sets-and-reps-to-build-muscle/).

## Technique tips

- **Control the lowering part** (2 to 3 seconds). That's where much of the growth stimulus comes from.
- **Full range of motion:** fully straighten your arms at the bottom of curls.
- **Elbows still** on curls and pushdowns. No swinging your body.
- **Squeeze at the top** for a second.

## Mistakes that stop arm growth

- **Too heavy, too sloppy:** swinging the weight takes the work off your arms.
- **Only biceps:** ignoring triceps, which are the bigger muscle.
- **Arms every day:** muscles grow during recovery.
- **Not eating enough:** arm size follows overall body weight and muscle gain. See [how to gain weight fast for skinny people](../how-to-gain-weight-fast-for-skinny-people/).
- **Skipping compound lifts:** bench press, overhead press, rows and pull-ups build your arms too.

## How fast can arms grow?

Beginners can see noticeable changes within 2 to 3 months of consistent training and eating. Measure your arm (relaxed and flexed) once a month to track progress honestly.

## Arm workout at home

No gym today? This works with a backpack filled with books or a bag of rice:

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Works |
|---|---|---|
| Backpack curls | 3 × 10–15 | Biceps |
| Diamond push-ups (or knees down) | 3 × as many as you can | Triceps |
| Chair dips | 3 × 10–15 | Triceps |
| Backpack hammer curls | 3 × 12 | Biceps, forearms |
| Towel isometric curl (pull against your foot) | 3 × 20 seconds | Biceps |

Make it harder each week by adding books, doing more reps or slowing down the lowering part.

## Why your arms aren't growing

1. **Not eating enough.** Arms are small muscles; if your body weight isn't moving up slowly, they won't grow much.
2. **Too little protein.** Aim for about 1.6 g per kg of body weight a day.
3. **Swinging the weight.** Momentum moves the weight, not your biceps.
4. **Ignoring triceps.** They make up about two-thirds of your upper arm.
5. **No progression.** The same dumbbells for 3 months means the same arms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many times a week should I train arms?

Twice a week works well for most people, either as separate arm work or added to upper body days.

### Are push-ups good for triceps?

Yes, especially close-grip (diamond) push-ups.

### Do I need heavy weights for big arms?

Moderate weights for 8 to 15 controlled reps, close to failure, work very well for arms.

### Can I build arms at home?

Yes, with dumbbells or resistance bands, push-up variations and chair dips.

### Why are my arms not growing?

Usually not enough food, not enough protein, poor form, or no progression. Fix those before changing exercises.
