The Everest Three Passes trek is one of the hardest teahouse treks in Nepal. **It crosses three passes above 5,300 m, with about 17 days of walking, rough rocky ground and long descents.** You need to be fit, experienced with long walking days, and patient with altitude. The shorter Gokyo Lakes trek is a gentler way to see the same region.

## The numbers

Altitudes from the itinerary of [Marron Treks](https://www.marrontreks.com/everest-three-passes-trek), a registered Nepali agency:

| Point | Altitude |
|---|---|
| Kongma La | about 5,535 m |
| Cho La | about 5,420 m |
| Renjo La | about 5,360 m |
| Gokyo Ri viewpoint | about 5,357 m |
| Kala Patthar | about 5,550 m |
| Everest Base Camp | about 5,364 m |

Their Three Passes itinerary runs 20 days in total, with about 17 days on foot, starting and ending at Lukla (about 2,840 m).

## Three Passes or Gokyo: which suits you?

| | Gokyo Lakes trek | Three Passes trek |
|---|---|---|
| Difficulty | Moderate to hard | Very hard |
| High passes | None needed (Gokyo Ri is a side climb) | Three, each above 5,300 m |
| Good for | Fit first-time trekkers at altitude | Experienced trekkers |
| Terrain | Mostly good trails | Rocky moraines, steep and icy sections |

If you have never trekked above 4,000 m, Gokyo or [Everest Base Camp](../everest-base-camp-trek-training-plan/) is a better first step.

## What makes the passes hard

- **Long pass days:** early starts, steep climbs, then long descents.
- **Rough ground:** loose rocks and moraine, sometimes snow or ice near the top of Cho La.
- **Days above 5,000 m** in a row, where everything feels harder and sleep is often poor.
- **Cold:** early starts in the dark mean freezing temperatures for the first hours.

## The 12-week plan

Five sessions a week: two climbs, two strength, one long walk (plus a second walk the next day from week 5).

| Weeks | Climb sessions | Long walk | Pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | 40 to 50 min stairs or steep incline | 3 hours | 3 to 5 kg |
| 4 | Easy week | 2 hours | 4 kg |
| 5 to 7 | 50 to 60 min, add hard climbing intervals | 5 hours, plus 3 hours next day | 6 to 7 kg |
| 8 | Easy week | 3 hours | 6 kg |
| 9 to 11 | 60 to 75 min | 6 to 7 hours, plus 4 hours next day | Trek pack |
| 12 | Short and easy | 2 hours | Trek pack |

**Climbing intervals** (from week 5): after a warm-up, climb hard for 3 to 4 minutes, then walk easy for 2 minutes. Repeat 5 to 6 times. This builds the strength to keep moving on steep pass climbs.

**Strength, twice a week:** step-ups and step-downs, goblet squats, Romanian deadlifts, lunges, calf raises, side planks. Strong legs and a stable core help on loose, uneven ground. For downhill strength, follow the exercises in [7 ways to stop knee pain when hiking downhill](../knee-pain-hiking-downhill/).

## Balance and footwork

Moraine and rocky passes punish clumsy feet. Add these twice a week:

- Single-leg balance, 30 seconds each leg, then with eyes closed
- Walking on uneven ground or trails whenever you can
- Practice with trekking poles on stairs and rough paths

## Altitude comes first

On a trek this high, altitude is a bigger risk than fitness. Fitness does not protect you from altitude sickness ([TravelHealthPro](https://travelhealthpro.org.uk/factsheets/altitude-illness)); a slow ascent does. Above 3,000 m, sleep no more than about 500 m higher each night and take a rest day every three or four days. Choose an itinerary with rest days built in, and be ready to skip a pass if anyone feels unwell. Read [altitude sickness on Nepal treks](../altitude-sickness-nepal-trek/).

## Food and calories

Pass days are long and cold, so you will burn a lot of energy. Eat a big breakfast, carry quick snacks, and use the [trekking calorie calculator](../../calculators/trekking-calorie-calculator/) to see your needs. More tips: [what to eat on a Nepal trek](../what-to-eat-on-nepal-trek/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How hard is the Everest Three Passes trek?
Very hard: about 17 days of walking with three passes above 5,300 m, rough ground and long days. It suits fit, experienced trekkers.

### What is the highest pass on the Three Passes trek?
Kongma La, at about 5,535 m.

### Is Gokyo easier than Everest Base Camp?
Both are demanding and reach similar heights. Gokyo is often quieter. Neither needs a high pass if you go there and back the same way.

### How long should I train for the Three Passes trek?
About 12 weeks if you are already active, with long back-to-back walks and plenty of climbing.

### Do I need crampons for Cho La?
Conditions change with the season. Ask your guide or agency about snow and ice before you go.
