Brown rice is a little better for weight loss, but not by much. **Per cooked cup, the two have almost the same calories. Brown rice has about four times more fibre, which can help you feel full.** For most people, how much rice you eat matters far more than which colour it is.

If you love white rice and hate brown, you do not need to switch. Keep white rice and make the portion smaller.

## The numbers side by side

USDA values for plain cooked long-grain rice, as shown on our food pages ([white rice](../../food/rice-white-calories/), [brown rice](../../food/rice-brown-calories/)):

| Per 100 g cooked | White rice | Brown rice |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | 130 kcal | 123 kcal |
| Protein | 2.7 g | 2.7 g |
| Carbs | 28.2 g | 25.6 g |
| Fat | 0.3 g | 1.0 g |
| Fibre | 0.4 g | 1.6 g |

A Nepali plate of rice is often around 300 g cooked. That is about 390 kcal of white rice or about 369 kcal of brown rice: a difference of roughly 20 kcal, the same as a few bites of rice.

## Where brown rice really helps

- **More fibre.** Brown rice keeps the outer layer of the grain, so it has more fibre. Fibre slows eating and helps many people feel full for longer.
- More chewing. Brown rice is firmer, so people tend to eat it more slowly.
- A little more of some minerals, because the outer layer is kept.

None of these makes brown rice a fat-burning food. They simply make it a bit easier to eat less.

## Where white rice is fine

- **It is what you will actually eat.** A diet you enjoy is a diet you can keep.
- Easy on the stomach. Many people find white rice easier to digest, especially after hard training or when unwell.
- Same protein. Neither rice is a protein food; that comes from dal, eggs, meat or paneer.

## How to eat rice and still lose weight

The plate matters more than the grain:

1. **Half the plate vegetables:** saag, tarkari, salad.
2. A quarter protein: thick dal, eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, soybean.
3. A quarter rice: one small plate, not two or three.

Swapping a second plate of rice for a second bowl of dal or tarkari does more for weight loss than switching to brown rice. ## Try a half-and-half mix

If brown rice tastes too heavy, cook half white and half brown. You get some of the fibre and keep most of the taste. Brown rice usually needs more water and longer cooking, so soak it for 30 minutes first, or cook the brown rice for a few minutes before adding the white.

## What about roti?

Roti is not automatically better than rice. A medium roti (40 g) is about 119 kcal ([roti calories](../../food/roti-calories/)). Two roti and a cup of rice are close in calories. Compare all the breads in [roti vs naan vs puri calories](../roti-vs-naan-vs-puri-calories/).

## How much rice should you eat?

It depends on your size, how active you are and your goal. As a starting point:

- **Losing weight:** one small plate (about 150 to 200 g cooked) per meal, with plenty of vegetables and protein.
- Maintaining: a normal plate, adjusted to how your weight changes.
- Building muscle or training hard: larger portions are fine, especially after training.

## Rice for gym training

Rice is one of the best foods for training, not something to fear. It gives quick, easy energy and digests well, which is why many lifters eat it before and after workouts.

- **Before training (1 to 3 hours):** a normal plate of rice with dal or a little chicken gives energy without feeling heavy. White rice is easier on the stomach before a session.
- After training: rice with a protein food, such as dal, eggs, chicken or paneer, refuels your muscles. See [what to eat after a workout](../what-to-eat-after-workout/).
- Building muscle: you may need more rice, not less. Larger portions help you eat enough calories to grow.

On rest days, many people simply eat a little less rice and keep the protein the same.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is brown rice better than white rice for weight loss?
Slightly, because it has more fibre and can help you feel full. The calories are almost the same, so the portion size matters more than the type.

### How many calories are in a cup of white rice?
About 205 kcal in a cup of cooked white rice (158 g), based on USDA data.

### Does white rice make you fat?
No single food makes you fat. Eating more energy than you use does. White rice fits a weight loss diet when the portion is sensible.

### Is brown rice good for diabetes?
Its extra fibre can help, but portion size still matters. If you have diabetes, ask your doctor or a dietitian how much rice suits you.

### Can I eat rice at night and still lose weight?
Yes. The total you eat across the day matters more than the time. A smaller rice portion at dinner works well for many people.
