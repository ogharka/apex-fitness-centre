The Manaslu Circuit is a hard trek, but you do not need to be an athlete to finish it. **You need legs that can walk 6 to 8 hours a day for about two weeks, and the patience to go up slowly.** The hardest day is the crossing of Larkya La pass at about 5,106 m, a very long day with a steep climb and an even longer descent.

This guide explains what makes Manaslu demanding and gives you a 10-week plan to get ready.

## Manaslu at a glance

Figures from the trek guide of [North Nepal Trek](https://northnepaltrek.com/manaslu-circuit-trek), a registered Nepali agency:

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Highest point | Larkya La pass, about 5,106 m |
| Highest night | Dharmasala (Larkya Phedi), about 4,460 m |
| Distance | About 177 km |
| Typical length | 11 to 14 days of walking, often more with rest days |
| Rules | Restricted area: you must trek with a licensed guide arranged through a registered agency |

Permit prices and rules change, so confirm them with your agency before you book.

## What makes Manaslu harder than it looks

Many trekkers compare it with the [Annapurna Circuit](../annapurna-circuit-trek-fitness/), and the two have a lot in common: both climb slowly through river valleys and both cross a pass above 5,000 m. Three things make Manaslu feel tougher for many people:

1. **More days in a row with no easy exit.** Villages are smaller and further apart, and there are fewer roads to escape on if you get tired or sick.
2. Larkya La day. You start before dawn from about 4,460 m, climb over 600 m to the pass, then walk a long way down the other side. Expect a very long day.
3. Basic teahouses. Food and beds are simpler, which makes recovery between days harder.

None of this needs special strength. It needs endurance, strong legs for the descents, and a pace you can keep for days.

## Are you ready? A quick self-test

Try these before you start training, and again two weeks before you fly:

- Walk 6 hours with breaks, then walk 3 to 4 hours the next day.
- Walk up and down 25 floors of stairs at a steady pace.
- Do 20 step-ups on each leg onto a knee-height step.
- Walk 2 hours with the backpack you will carry.
- Walk down 15 floors of stairs without knee pain.

If two or more feel very hard, give yourself the full 10 weeks below.

## The 10-week Manaslu plan

Do two climb sessions, two strength sessions and one long walk every week. Keep the climbs at a pace where you can still talk in short sentences.

| Week | Climb sessions (x2) | Weekend long walk | Pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | 30 to 40 min of stairs or an incline treadmill | 2 to 3 hours | None, then 3 kg |
| 3 to 4 | 40 to 50 min | 3 to 4 hours | 4 to 5 kg |
| 5 | Easier week: 30 min | 2.5 hours | 4 kg |
| 6 to 7 | 50 to 60 min, add a few faster climbs | 5 hours, plus 2 hours the next day | 6 kg |
| 8 to 9 | 60 min | 6 hours, plus 3 hours the next day | Your trek pack |
| 10 | Easy: 30 min | 2 hours | Trek pack |

Back-to-back walks in weeks 6 to 9 matter most. A long trek is many days in a row, so your legs must learn to walk when they are already tired.

**Strength, twice a week:** step-ups with a slow step down, squats, reverse lunges, calf raises and a plank. Three sets of 8 to 12. The slow step down trains the muscles that protect your knees on long descents. New to squats? Start with [how to squat properly](../how-to-squat-properly/).

## Altitude: the part training cannot fix

Being fit does not protect you from altitude sickness. The UK's official travel health service says fitness "does not appear to protect against altitude illness" ([TravelHealthPro](https://travelhealthpro.org.uk/factsheets/altitude-illness)). What protects you is going up slowly: above 3,000 m, sleep no more than about 500 m higher each night and take a rest day every three or four days.

Good Manaslu itineraries include acclimatisation days around Samagaon. Do not skip them, even if you feel strong. Read our full guide to [altitude sickness on Nepal treks](../altitude-sickness-nepal-trek/).

## Food on the trail

Long trek days burn far more energy than a normal day, and appetite often drops higher up. Eat at every meal and snack between teahouses. Dal bhat is the best everyday fuel: see [what to eat on a Nepal trek](../what-to-eat-on-nepal-trek/), and estimate your needs with the [trekking calorie calculator](../../calculators/trekking-calorie-calculator/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How difficult is the Manaslu Circuit?
It is a strenuous trek: about two weeks of long walking days in remote country, with Larkya La pass at about 5,106 m. Fit, prepared beginners finish it, but it is not a good first trek for someone who has never walked for days in a row.

### Is Manaslu harder than the Annapurna Circuit?
Many trekkers find it harder because it is more remote, has simpler teahouses and fewer ways out. The altitude and the pass are similar.

### Can I do the Manaslu Circuit without a guide?
No. It is a restricted area, and you must trek with a licensed guide through a registered agency. Check the current rules before you book.

### How long should I train for Manaslu?
About 10 weeks if you are already active, longer if you are starting from little exercise.

### What is the hardest day on the Manaslu Circuit?
Larkya La day: an early start from about 4,460 m, a climb to about 5,106 m, then a long descent.
