Leg day has a reputation for being the day everyone skips, but your legs and glutes are the **biggest muscles in your body**. Training them builds strength, burns a lot of energy, improves athletic performance and makes everyday life (stairs, hikes, lifting things) easier. Here's a simple leg session that works for complete beginners.

## The beginner leg workout

Do this **once or twice a week**, or as part of full-body sessions.

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Main muscles |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat or back squat | 3 x 8–10 | Thighs, glutes |
| Romanian deadlift (dumbbells) | 3 x 10 | Hamstrings, glutes |
| Leg press | 3 x 10–12 | Thighs, glutes |
| Walking lunges | 2 x 10 each leg | Thighs, glutes, balance |
| Leg curl machine | 2 x 12 | Hamstrings |
| Standing calf raises | 3 x 15 | Calves |

Rest **90 seconds to 2 minutes** between sets of the big lifts and about a minute for the smaller ones.

## Exercise tips

### Squat

Feet shoulder-width, sit down and back, knees following your toes, back flat. See our full [squat form guide](../how-to-squat-properly/).

### Romanian deadlift

Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs, soft knees, push your hips back until you feel a stretch in your hamstrings, then stand up. Keep your back flat. More in our [deadlift guide](../how-to-deadlift-for-beginners/).

### Leg press

Feet hip-width on the platform. Lower until your knees are at about 90 degrees or a little deeper, **without your lower back lifting** off the seat. Don't lock your knees hard at the top.

### Walking lunges

Long step forward, back knee towards the floor, front knee over the middle of your foot. Push through the front heel to step forward into the next lunge.

### Calf raises

Full range: stretch at the bottom, pause at the top. Calves respond well to slow, controlled reps.

## How to progress

- When you reach the top of the rep range with good form on all sets, **add a small amount of weight** next session.
- Write down your weights. Leg strength often improves quickly in the first months.
- Read [how many sets and reps to build muscle](../how-many-sets-and-reps-to-build-muscle/) to understand why this matters.

## Dealing with leg-day soreness

Your first few leg sessions will probably make stairs difficult for a day or two. That's normal (it's called DOMS) and gets much better after 2 to 3 weeks. To reduce it:

- **Start light** in the first sessions.
- **Walk** the next day.
- **Eat protein and sleep well.**

See [muscle soreness after the gym](../muscle-soreness-after-gym/).

## Don't skip legs if you want...

- **Fat loss:** big muscles use a lot of energy.
- **A balanced physique:** big arms with skinny legs is a classic beginner look.
- **Better sports performance:** running, football, cricket, kabaddi.
- **Trekking:** strong legs make Nepal's hills and mountains far more enjoyable. See our [Everest Base Camp training plan](../everest-base-camp-trek-training-plan/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should beginners train legs?

Once or twice a week is enough for most beginners.

### Is leg press as good as squats?

Both are useful. Squats train balance and core more; the leg press lets you push your legs safely with less technique. Many people do both.

### Will leg training make my legs bulky?

Most people, especially women, build lean, strong legs rather than bulky ones. Size depends on training volume, genetics and diet.

### My knees hurt when I squat. What should I do?

Use lighter weight, check your form, and try box squats or leg press with a comfortable range. If pain continues, see a doctor or physiotherapist.
