A good breakfast for weight loss has two things: **protein to keep you full and a sensible amount of energy.** In Nepal, the easiest options are eggs, curd, oats, chana and milk, not biscuits and sweet tea. Pick one of the seven ideas below and you will reach lunch less hungry.

All numbers come from USDA FoodData Central, as shown on our [food calorie chart](../../food/).

## Why breakfast matters when you are losing weight

You do not have to eat breakfast to lose weight. Weight loss depends on what you eat across the whole day. But if you get very hungry by 10 AM and then eat a huge khana or a plate of samosa, a small protein breakfast can make the rest of the day easier. Choose the pattern that keeps you in control.

## 7 breakfast ideas

| Breakfast | Calories | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| 2 boiled eggs + black tea | about 142 kcal | about 12.5 g |
| 1 katori plain curd + 1 banana | about 197 kcal | about 6.5 g |
| Oats (40 g) cooked in 1 glass milk | about 301 kcal | about 13 g |
| 2 boiled eggs + 1 roti | about 261 kcal | about 17 g |
| 1 katori chana + cucumber | about 330 kcal | about 12 g |
| 1 glass milk + 1 handful peanuts | about 313 kcal | about 14.5 g |
| Egg omelette (2 eggs) + vegetables | about 160 to 200 kcal | about 12.5 g |

*Omelette depends on the oil you use: each teaspoon adds about 40 kcal.*

### 1. Boiled eggs and black tea
The simplest option. Two large eggs give about 12.5 g of protein for about 142 kcal ([egg calories](../../food/egg-calories/)). Black tea with little or no sugar keeps it light.

### 2. Curd and banana
Plain curd gives protein and a banana adds quick energy. One katori (150 g) of curd is about 92 kcal ([curd calories](../../food/curd-calories/)). Choose plain, not sweetened.

### 3. Oats cooked in milk
Filling and warm on cold mornings. 40 g of dry oats plus a glass of whole milk is about 300 kcal and 13 g of protein. Use water for half the liquid if you want it lighter ([oats calories](../../food/oats-calories/)).

### 4. Eggs and one roti
For people who feel hungry without something like bread. One medium roti adds about 119 kcal ([roti calories](../../food/roti-calories/)).

### 5. Chana
Boiled or lightly cooked chana with cucumber, onion and lemon. It is high in fibre and very filling, but higher in calories when cooked in oil, so keep it to one katori ([chana calories](../../food/chana-calories/)).

### 6. Milk and peanuts
Quick for busy mornings. Count the peanuts: one handful (28 g) is about 164 kcal ([peanut calories](../../food/peanuts-calories/)), and the packet goes fast.

### 7. Vegetable omelette
Two eggs with onion, tomato, spinach and green chilli. Use one teaspoon of oil and a non-stick pan.

## Breakfasts that slow weight loss

- **Sweet milk tea with biscuits:** easy to have 2 or 3 cups and a packet of biscuits without feeling full. Three cups of milk tea alone is over 350 kcal ([chai calories](../../food/chai-calories/)).
- Fried snacks: samosa, puri, pakora and sel roti are high in oil.
- Instant noodles: low in protein and easy to overeat.
- Big bowls of sweetened cereal or muesli: check the sugar.

Enjoy them sometimes; just do not make them the daily habit.

## Making it work with the 9 AM khana

Many Nepali families eat a big khana around 9 or 10 AM. In that case, keep the early breakfast very small, such as two eggs or a katori of curd, and use the plate method at khana: half vegetables, a quarter protein, a quarter rice. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Nepali breakfast for weight loss?
Two boiled eggs with black tea, or a katori of plain curd with fruit. Both give protein for few calories and keep you full.

### Is milk tea bad for weight loss?
Not if you keep it to one or two cups with little sugar. Three or more sweet cups a day, with biscuits, adds up quickly.

### Are oats good for weight loss?
Yes. Oats are filling and high in fibre. Watch the portion: 40 g dry is a normal bowl.

### Should I skip breakfast to lose weight?
You can if it suits you, but if skipping makes you overeat later, a small protein breakfast is better.

### How many eggs can I eat for breakfast?
Two or three eggs is a common, filling breakfast for healthy adults. See [how many eggs can you eat a day](../how-many-eggs-per-day/).
