For most healthy adults, **1 to 3 whole eggs a day** fits well into a balanced diet, and many lifters eat more without problems. Eggs are one of the cheapest, easiest and highest-quality protein foods in Nepal. The cholesterol worry has softened a lot in recent years, but if you have **high cholesterol, diabetes or heart disease**, ask your doctor what amount suits you.

## What is in one egg?

A large egg contains roughly:

- **About 6 g of protein**, high quality, with all essential amino acids
- **About 70 to 80 calories**
- **About 5 g of fat**, mostly in the yolk
- Vitamins and minerals including **B12, vitamin D, selenium and choline**, mostly in the yolk

## Are eggs bad for cholesterol?

Egg yolks contain cholesterol, and for years people were told to strictly limit them. Research since then shows that, for most people, **cholesterol in food has a smaller effect on blood cholesterol** than once thought. Saturated fat, refined carbs, weight, activity and genetics matter a lot.

That is why many health authorities removed strict daily cholesterol limits for the general population, while still advising a balanced diet. Some people, however, are more sensitive to dietary cholesterol. If your blood cholesterol is high, follow your doctor's advice.

What you eat **with** eggs often matters more: eggs fried in lots of oil or ghee with sausages, white bread and sweet tea are a very different meal from boiled eggs with roti and vegetables.

## How many eggs for gym-goers?

| Your situation | Reasonable starting point |
|---|---|
| Healthy adult, normal diet | 1–3 whole eggs a day |
| Lifter needing more protein | 2–3 whole eggs + extra egg whites, or other protein foods |
| High cholesterol or heart disease | Ask your doctor; many are advised to limit yolks |

Remember your total protein target. Eggs are great, but variety is better: dal, curd, paneer, chicken, fish and soybean all help. See [how much protein you need](../how-much-protein-do-you-need/).

## Whole eggs or egg whites?

- **Whole eggs:** more nutrients, more filling, more calories.
- **Egg whites:** almost pure protein (about 3 to 4 g per white), very few calories.

A good balance for many people trying to lose fat: **2 whole eggs + 2 to 3 extra whites**.

## 8 easy egg meals

1. **Boiled eggs** with salt, pepper and timur
2. **Egg bhurji** (scrambled with onion, tomato, chilli) and roti
3. **Egg curry** with rice
4. **Omelette** with spinach, onion and tomato
5. **Egg fried rice** with plenty of vegetables and little oil
6. **Egg sandwich** with brown bread
7. **Anda chiura**: boiled eggs with beaten rice and achar
8. **Masala omelette** wrapped in a roti

## Is it safe to eat raw eggs?

Better not. Raw eggs carry a small risk of salmonella food poisoning, and cooked egg protein is digested better. Cook eggs until the white is set.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I eat 4 or 5 eggs a day?

Many healthy people do without problems, but variety in protein is better. If you have high cholesterol or other health conditions, ask your doctor.

### Are eggs good for building muscle?

Yes. They provide high-quality protein that supports muscle repair and growth along with training.

### Is it better to eat eggs in the morning?

Any time works. Eggs at breakfast help many people feel full until lunch.

### Brown eggs or white eggs: which is better?

Nutritionally they are very similar. The shell colour depends on the hen breed. Local eggs and farm eggs are both good choices.
