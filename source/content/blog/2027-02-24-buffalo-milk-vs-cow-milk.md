Buffalo milk has more of everything than cow milk: more calories, more fat and a little more protein. **One glass of buffalo milk has about 237 calories and 9.2 g of protein, compared with about 149 calories and 7.7 g of protein in a glass of whole cow milk.** That makes buffalo milk better for gaining weight and cow milk the lighter choice for losing it.

In Nepal, both are common, and many households drink buffalo milk without knowing how much richer it is.

## Per glass, side by side

USDA values for one glass (244 g, about 1 cup), as shown on our food pages ([buffalo milk](../../food/buffalo-milk-calories/), [cow milk](../../food/milk-calories/)):

| Per glass (244 g) | Buffalo milk | Whole cow milk |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | 237 kcal | 149 kcal |
| Protein | 9.2 g | 7.7 g |
| Fat | 16.8 g | 7.9 g |
| Carbs | 12.6 g | 11.7 g |

The big difference is fat: buffalo milk has roughly twice as much. Fat is why buffalo milk tastes creamier and why it makes thick curd, paneer and ghee.

## Which is better for weight loss?

**Cow milk.** A glass has about 88 fewer calories than buffalo milk. If you drink two glasses a day, switching saves about 176 calories a day, which adds up over weeks.

Other easy ways to cut milk calories:

- Choose toned or low-fat milk if your dairy sells it.
- Use less milk in tea. Three cups of milk tea a day is already over 350 calories ([chai calories](../../food/chai-calories/)).
- Drink plain water when you are thirsty, and keep milk for meals.

## Which is better for weight gain?

**Buffalo milk.** If you are skinny and struggle to eat enough, a glass of buffalo milk is an easy 237 calories and 9 g of protein. Two glasses a day add almost 500 calories without a big meal. Read more in [how to gain weight fast for skinny people](../how-to-gain-weight-fast-for-skinny-people/).

## Which is better for building muscle?

Both are good protein sources. Per glass, buffalo milk gives a little more protein (9.2 g vs 7.7 g), but also much more fat.

- **Bulking or a hard gainer:** buffalo milk helps you reach your calories.
- **Building muscle while staying lean:** cow milk gives protein with fewer calories.

Either way, milk adds to your daily protein. Most people who exercise do well on about 1.4 to 2.0 g of protein per kg of body weight a day ([ISSN position stand](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28642676/)). Check your target with the [protein calculator](../../calculators/protein-calculator/).

## Curd from buffalo or cow milk

Curd follows the milk it is made from. Buffalo curd is thicker and richer; cow curd is lighter. If you eat a lot of curd and want to lose weight, cow milk curd is the lighter choice. Plain curd made from whole cow milk is about 92 calories per katori ([curd calories](../../food/curd-calories/)).

## Is buffalo milk healthy?

Yes, for most people. It is nutritious and rich in protein and calcium. The extra fat and calories are not a problem in themselves; they simply need to fit your total intake. People who have been told to limit saturated fat by their doctor should ask about the right milk for them.

## How milk adds up in a day

Milk often hides in tea, curd and sweets, so it is easy to drink more than you think. Here is an example of a normal day:

| Where the milk goes | Buffalo milk | Whole cow milk |
|---|---|---|
| Milk in 3 cups of tea (about 50 ml each) | about 145 kcal | about 92 kcal |
| One glass at breakfast | about 237 kcal | about 149 kcal |
| Total | about 382 kcal | about 241 kcal |

Switching that day from buffalo to cow milk saves about 140 calories, without changing anything else you eat. If you are trying to lose weight, that is an easy first change. If you are trying to gain, keep the buffalo milk and add a second glass.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many calories are in a glass of buffalo milk?
About 237 calories in one glass (244 g), with 9.2 g of protein and 16.8 g of fat, based on USDA data.

### Does buffalo milk have more protein than cow milk?
A little: about 9.2 g per glass compared with 7.7 g for whole cow milk.

### Is buffalo milk good for weight loss?
It is higher in calories than cow milk, so cow milk or toned milk is usually the better choice when you are trying to lose weight.

### Is buffalo milk good for gaining weight?
Yes. Its extra calories and fat make it an easy way to add energy if you struggle to eat enough.

### Can I drink milk after the gym?
Yes. Milk gives protein and carbs, which fit well after training. See [what to eat after a workout](../what-to-eat-after-workout/).
