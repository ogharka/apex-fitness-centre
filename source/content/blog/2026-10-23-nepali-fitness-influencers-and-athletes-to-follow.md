## Coaches and strength trainers

### Bramand "Tyson" Moktan

A competitive powerlifter and strength coach, and the founder of the **Ox Strength** coaching system. He has coached at Gymkhana since 2017 and co-runs the **Samaya** YouTube channel.

**Follow for:** strength training, powerlifting and functional fitness.
**Instagram:** [@tysonmoktann](https://www.instagram.com/tysonmoktann/)

## Athletes who made history

These athletes are better known for competition results than for social media, but every Nepali lifter should know their names.

### Nanita Maharjan

A bodybuilder from Kirtipur who started training in 2013. At the **8th WBPF World Bodybuilding and Physique Sports Championships** in Pattaya, Thailand, in December 2016, she won **bronze** in the athlete physique (165 cm) category, reported as [Nepal's first medal at that event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanita_Maharjan).

### Binu Gharti

At the **16th South Asian Bodybuilding and Physique Sports Championship** in Thimphu, Bhutan, in July 2026, Binu Gharti [won gold in women's swimwear and silver in model physique](https://www.hamropatro.com/en/news/detail/774803045513786).

### Nepal's powerlifting team

Nepal regularly sends lifters to international powerlifting competitions. At the World Powerlifting Championship held in Amravati, India, in December 2020, Nepal won [three gold medals and one silver](https://thehimalayantimes.com/kathmandu/nepal-bags-three-gold-one-silver-in-world-power-lifting-championship/), through Hasan Gurung, Ashish Ale Magar, Shruti Karki and Rajesh Suwal.

## How to get the most out of fitness influencers

- **Follow coaches who explain why**, not just show workouts. Understanding the reason behind an exercise makes you better at training on your own.
- **Be careful with supplement promotions.** Many creators are paid by brands. That is normal, but it means a product post is an advert, not independent advice.
- **Do not compare your day 30 with their year 10.** Most of the people above have trained for many years.
- **Beware of "transformation in 30 days" claims**, steroid-like results promised to beginners, and anyone selling miracle fat burners.
