HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE (this file is never published because its name starts with _ )

1. Copy everything in this file.
2. In source/content/blog, tap "Add file" > "Create new file".
3. Name it like: 2026-11-02-best-time-to-train.md  (date, then the web address you want, with dashes)
4. Paste, change the lines at the top, write your post below the second --- line.
5. Change "draft: true" to "draft: false" (or delete that line), then tap "Commit changes".
   The post is live about 2 minutes later at apexfitnesscentre.shop/blog/best-time-to-train/

THE LINES AT THE TOP
- title, description, date: required. Date format is YYYY-MM-DD.
  A future date schedules the post: it goes live by itself on that morning.
- category: use one of: Supplements, Training, Diet, Weight loss, Gyms in Nepal, Fitness careers, Influencers and athletes, Competitions
- seo_title: optional shorter title for Google (under 60 characters) if your title is long
- FAQ: end the post with a section called  ## Frequently asked questions  and put each question as  ### Question?  with the answer under it. Google reads these automatically.
- image: optional cover photo. Upload it to source/content/blog/images/ first, then write just the name, e.g. image: squat-form.jpg
- image_alt: describe the photo in a few words, e.g. Member doing a barbell squat at Apex
- No photo? Leave image empty. A branded cover is made automatically from your title.
  Add cover_stat (a big number, e.g. 3-5 g) and cover_label (a few words under it) to make it stronger,
  or cover_photo: photo.jpg to use one of your photos from the images folder on the cover.
- Add 1 or 2 photos inside the post too (real gym photos are best). Every photo needs a description in the [ ] part.
- products: optional. Product web names separated by commas, shown as shop cards under the post, e.g.
  products: muscleblaze-biozyme-performance-whey-2kg-french-vanilla-creme, muscleblaze-creatine-monohydrate-320g-citrus-blast
