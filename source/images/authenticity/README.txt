Authenticity proof photos (supplements only, real photos from your own stock, never stock images).

Name each photo <product-slug>-<slot>.webp (or .jpg / .jpeg), about 800 px wide:
  <slug>-seal      the sealed pack, as it arrives
  <slug>-batch     close-up of the batch code and expiry date
  <slug>-importer  the importer sticker or authenticity QR code

Example: scitron-volcano-pre-workout-seal.jpg
Product slugs are the folder names under /products/ on the site.
Each photo appears in the "Authenticity proof" box next to the WhatsApp button on the next build.
The box stays hidden on a product until at least one real photo exists.
