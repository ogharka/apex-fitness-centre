About Apex Fitness Centre

Apex Fitness Centre is a gym in Urlabari-9, Durgapuri, Morang, Nepal. We are open 4:30 AM to 8:30 PM, Sunday to Friday (closed on Saturday), with trainers on the floor and a front desk to help members.

Next to the gym we run this online store. We sell authentic supplements bought from authorised sellers, with free delivery all over Nepal. Orders come to us on WhatsApp, and a real person from the gym confirms stock, payment and delivery.

The blog and guides are written by our team from what members ask us every day: how to start training, how much protein you need, which supplements are worth the money and how to avoid fakes. We keep advice simple and honest, and we do not make medical claims.
