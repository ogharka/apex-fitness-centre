Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31. This policy covers apexfitnesscentre.shop, run by Apex Fitness Centre, Urlabari-9, Durgapuri, Morang, Nepal.

What we collect

We do not have accounts or sign-ups on this website. When you order or ask a question, you send us a message on WhatsApp. That message, with your name, phone number and delivery address if you give them, is used only to answer you, deliver your order and keep simple sales records. We do not sell it to anyone.

Your cart

Your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you send the WhatsApp message yourself.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used, for example how many people tap Order on WhatsApp. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your device, browser, approximate location and the pages you view. It does not give us your name or phone number.

Advertising

We show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings, and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site still works. To ask us to delete messages or details you sent us, message us on WhatsApp at 9708819605.

Health information

Articles on this site are general information, not medical advice. Ask a doctor if you have a health condition.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
