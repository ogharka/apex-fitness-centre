Mutton Biryani Calories and Protein

1 cup (196 g) has about 282 calories and 16.5 g of protein. Biryani with meat, goat biryani.

282calories
16.5 gprotein
23.7 gcarbs
13.2 gfat

Mutton biryani is richer than chicken biryani because red meat and extra ghee add fat. USDA's meat biryani has about 144 calories per 100 g, about 40% more than chicken biryani.

Calories come from: Protein 24% · Carbs 34% · Fat 43%.

To get 25 g of protein from mutton biryani alone you would need about 298 g, which is 429 kcal.

Is mutton biryani good for weight loss?

A special-occasion meal. A cup-sized portion with salad is a reasonable way to enjoy it.

Is it good for building muscle?

Good protein and plenty of energy, but fattier than chicken.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Fried onions (birista) and ghee add a lot of calories.
  • Pair with a big salad to fill up.
  • Leftovers? Keep portions to one cup.
