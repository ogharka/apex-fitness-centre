Curd (Dahi, Plain Yoghurt) Calories and Protein

1 katori (150 g) has about 92 calories and 5.2 g of protein. Dahi, plain yoghurt, whole-milk curd.

92calories
5.2 gprotein
7 gcarbs
4.9 gfat

Plain curd (dahi) made from whole milk has about 61 calories and 3.5 g of protein per 100 g. Thick, strained curd (like Greek yoghurt or Nepali juju dhau) has more protein, and juju dhau also has added sugar.

Is curd good for weight loss?

A good side for most meals. It adds protein and makes spicy food easier to enjoy. Choose plain over sweetened.

Is it good for building muscle?

Useful, but you'd need a lot for big protein. Strained curd or hung curd roughly doubles the protein per spoon.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Hang curd in a cloth for 2–3 hours to make thick, high-protein curd.
  • Raita with cucumber is a light, cooling side.
  • Juju dhau is delicious but high in sugar: a treat, not a protein food.
