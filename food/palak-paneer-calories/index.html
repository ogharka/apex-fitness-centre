Palak Paneer Calories and Protein

1 cup (200 g) has about 192 calories and 10.5 g of protein. Saag paneer, spinach with cottage cheese.

192calories
10.5 gprotein
8.6 gcarbs
13.7 gfat

Palak paneer is cubes of paneer in a spiced spinach gravy. USDA's recipe has about 96 calories and 5 g of protein per 100 g. Paneer itself typically has about 18 g of protein per 100 g, so more paneer per bowl means more protein.

Calories come from: Protein 21% · Carbs 17% · Fat 62%.

To get 25 g of protein from palak paneer alone you would need about 478 g, which is 459 kcal.

Is palak paneer good for weight loss?

A good vegetarian main if cooked without much cream or butter.

Is it good for building muscle?

A useful vegetarian protein dish. Add extra paneer cubes or a side of dal.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Restaurant versions often add cream: ask for less.
  • Spinach adds iron, folate and fibre for few calories.
  • Low-fat paneer or tofu cuts the fat.
