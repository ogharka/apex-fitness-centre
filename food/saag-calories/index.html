Saag (Mustard Greens) Calories and Protein

1 cup cooked (140 g) has about 36 calories and 3.6 g of protein. Rayo ko saag, sarson ka saag, leafy greens.

36calories
3.6 gprotein
6.3 gcarbs
0.7 gfat

Saag (leafy greens, most often mustard greens or rayo in Nepal) is very low in calories and rich in vitamins A, C and K. Boiled, it has only about 26 calories per 100 g. The oil or ghee used to sauté it usually adds more calories than the greens.

Is saag good for weight loss?

Excellent. Fill half your plate with saag and other vegetables.

Is it good for building muscle?

Not a protein source, but important for vitamins, minerals and digestion.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • One tsp of oil to sauté a bowl of saag adds about 40 kcal.
  • Spinach is similar: about 23 kcal per 100 g cooked.
  • Combine with dal for a filling, low-calorie meal.
