Chicken Breast Calories and Protein

100 g cooked has about 165 calories and 31 g of protein. Boneless chicken breast, cooked.

165calories
31 gprotein
0 gcarbs
3.6 gfat

Cooked skinless chicken breast is one of the leanest protein foods: about 31 g of protein and 165 calories per 100 g. Chicken thighs and pieces with skin are tastier but have more fat.

Calories come from: Protein 79% · Carbs 0% · Fat 21%.

To get 25 g of protein from chicken breast alone you would need about 81 g, which is 133 kcal.

Is chicken breast good for weight loss?

Excellent. High protein, low fat, very filling. Grill, roast or cook in a light curry.

Is it good for building muscle?

One of the best muscle-building foods. 150 g cooked gives over 45 g of protein.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Raw chicken loses about a quarter of its weight when cooked; weigh cooked if you track.
  • Marinate in curd and spices for tender tandoori-style chicken.
  • Chicken curry made with lots of gravy has much less protein per bowl than plain breast.
