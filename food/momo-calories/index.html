Momo (Steamed) Calories and Protein

1 plate of 10 momo (290 g) has about 394 calories and 24 g of protein. Steamed momo, dumplings, veg or meat momo.

394calories
24 gprotein
38.5 gcarbs
16 gfat

Momo are Nepal's favourite food: thin wheat dumplings filled with minced meat or vegetables, steamed and served with achar. Using the closest USDA match, one steamed momo has about 40 calories, and a plate of 10 has about 390 calories and 24 g of protein.

Calories come from: Protein 24% · Carbs 39% · Fat 37%.

To get 25 g of protein from momo alone you would need about 302 g, which is 411 kcal.

Is momo good for weight loss?

Steamed momo can fit a fat-loss diet: a plate has a fair amount of protein. The problems are fried, kothey and chilli momo, extra plates and sugary drinks.

Is it good for building muscle?

A decent protein-and-carb meal. Chicken or buff momo give more protein than veg momo.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Steamed is best; fried momo add roughly 50% more calories.
  • Tomato achar is low in calories; sesame-heavy achar adds more.
  • Have a salad or soup with half a plate instead of a full plate.
