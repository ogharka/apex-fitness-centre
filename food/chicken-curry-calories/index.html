Chicken Curry Calories and Protein

1 cup with gravy (236 g) has about 194 calories and 14.9 g of protein. Kukhura ko masu, chicken masala.

194calories
14.9 gprotein
15.7 gcarbs
8.3 gfat

Chicken curry varies hugely from home to restaurant. USDA's recipe (meat with plenty of sauce and vegetables) averages 82 calories per 100 g. Restaurant curries with cream, butter or lots of oil can be two or three times higher.

To get 25 g of protein from chicken curry alone you would need about 396 g, which is 325 kcal.

Is chicken curry good for weight loss?

Home-style chicken curry with a moderate amount of oil is a great main dish. Take more chicken pieces and less gravy.

Is it good for building muscle?

Good protein, especially if you serve yourself more meat than gravy.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Butter chicken and korma usually have cream and butter: much higher in calories.
  • Skim visible oil from the top of the pot.
  • Use bone-in pieces for flavour with less oil.
