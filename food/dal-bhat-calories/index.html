Dal Bhat (Full Plate) Calories and Protein

1 typical plate has about 794 calories and 28.1 g of protein. Nepali thali, dal bhat tarkari.

794calories
28.1 gprotein
127.6 gcarbs
19.7 gfat

Dal bhat is Nepal's national meal: rice, lentil soup and vegetables, often with achar, and sometimes meat or curd. There is no single USDA entry for it, so we add up a typical plate from USDA values for each part: 2 cups of rice, 1 cup of dal and 1 cup of saag. That plate comes to about 790 calories and 28 g of protein.

Add achar (a few calories), a fried papad (about 40–60 kcal), a katori of curd (about 90 kcal, 5 g protein) or a meat curry to match your own plate.

Is dal bhat good for weight loss?

Dal bhat is balanced. To lose weight, keep the dal and vegetables and reduce the rice: going from 2 cups to 1 cup saves about 200 kcal.

Is it good for building muscle?

A great training meal. Add an egg, chicken, fish or curd to push protein higher.
