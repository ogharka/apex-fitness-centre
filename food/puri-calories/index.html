Puri (Poori) Calories and Protein

1 puri (36 g) has about 140 calories and 2.2 g of protein. Poori, deep-fried bread.

140calories
2.2 gprotein
13.9 gcarbs
8.7 gfat

Puri is a small wheat bread that puffs up when deep-fried. Because it absorbs oil, it has about three times the fat of roti per 100 g. Each puri has about 140 calories, and they are easy to eat four or six at a time.

To get 25 g of protein from puri alone you would need about 408 g, which is 1,593 kcal.

Is puri good for weight loss?

One of the highest-calorie breads. Keep puri for festivals and special breakfasts, and count them.

Is it good for building muscle?

Calorie-dense but low in protein. Not a good regular choice even when bulking.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Four puris with aloo tarkari can easily pass 800 kcal.
  • Drain on paper towels to remove some surface oil.
  • Pair with chana instead of potato curry for more protein and fibre.
