Naan Calories and Protein

1 piece (90 g) has about 262 calories and 8.7 g of protein. Plain naan, tandoori naan.

262calories
8.7 gprotein
45.4 gcarbs
5.1 gfat

Naan is a soft leavened flatbread baked in a tandoor, made with refined flour (maida), yoghurt and often butter. It is denser and larger than roti, so one naan usually has more than double the calories of a home roti.

To get 25 g of protein from naan alone you would need about 260 g, which is 756 kcal.

Is naan good for weight loss?

Fine occasionally, but large. One restaurant naan can be 350–400 kcal before butter or garlic butter. Share one, or choose roti instead.

Is it good for building muscle?

Easy extra calories for people who struggle to eat enough, but low in fibre. Combine with a protein-rich curry.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Butter naan and garlic naan add roughly 50–100 kcal more than plain naan.
  • Whole wheat naan has about twice the fibre of plain naan.
  • Ordering takeaway? One naan between two people is usually plenty with rice.
