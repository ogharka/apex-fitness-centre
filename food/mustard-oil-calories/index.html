Mustard Oil Calories and Protein

Mustard oil is the traditional cooking oil in Nepal, Bengal and North India. Like all oils, it has about 40 calories per teaspoon and 120 per tablespoon. Cooking oil is often the biggest hidden source of calories in South Asian meals.
