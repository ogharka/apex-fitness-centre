Pakora (Pakoda) Calories and Protein

1 pakora (12 g) has about 15 calories and 0.6 g of protein. Bhajiya, vegetable fritters.

15calories
0.6 gprotein
1.9 gcarbs
0.6 gfat

Pakora are vegetables dipped in spiced chickpea-flour (besan) batter and deep-fried. USDA's vegetable pakora average 123 calories per 100 g, about 15 per small piece. Larger restaurant pakora absorb more oil.

Is pakora good for weight loss?

Easy to overeat with tea. Count them, and keep them to a weekend snack.

Is it good for building muscle?

Besan adds a little protein, but most of the calories come from carbs and oil.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Air-fried or baked pakora use far less oil.
  • Onion and spinach pakora are among the lightest.
  • Paneer pakora add protein, but also more fat.
