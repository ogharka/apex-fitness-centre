Chicken Chowmein Calories and Protein

1 cup (about 220 g) has about 187 calories and 14.9 g of protein. Chow mein, chau chau, fried noodles.

187calories
14.9 gprotein
18.2 gcarbs
6.2 gfat

Chowmein is a hugely popular street and restaurant dish in Nepal and India: stir-fried noodles with vegetables and meat. USDA's restaurant chicken chow mein has about 85 calories per 100 g, but Nepali and Indian street versions often use more noodles and oil.

To get 25 g of protein from chicken chowmein alone you would need about 370 g, which is 314 kcal.

Is chicken chowmein good for weight loss?

Fine as an occasional meal. Ask for extra vegetables and less oil, and keep the portion to one plate.

Is it good for building muscle?

Low in protein for its volume unless you add extra chicken or egg.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Egg chowmein is an easy protein upgrade.
  • Instant noodles are much higher in fat and salt than fresh chowmein.
  • Street plates are often much bigger than they look.
