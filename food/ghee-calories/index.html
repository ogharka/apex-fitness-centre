Ghee Calories and Protein

1 teaspoon (5 g) has about 45 calories and 0 g of protein. Clarified butter, ghiu.

45calories
0 gprotein
0 gcarbs
5 gfat

Ghee is pure butterfat with the milk solids removed. Like all fats and oils, it is very calorie-dense: about 45 calories per teaspoon and 125 per tablespoon. It has no protein or carbs.

Is ghee good for weight loss?

Small amounts are fine and add great flavour, but it is easy to add 200–300 kcal a day without noticing. Measure it.

Is it good for building muscle?

An easy way to add calories when trying to gain weight, without making meals bigger.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Use a teaspoon, not a free pour.
  • Ghee is mostly saturated fat; health bodies suggest keeping saturated fat moderate.
  • Its high smoke point makes it good for tadka, but a little goes a long way.
