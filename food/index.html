How to use this chart

  • Losing weight? Find your calorie target with the calorie deficit calculator, then watch the big-ticket items: rice portions, cooking oil, ghee, fried snacks and sugary tea.
  • Building muscle? Use the protein calculator and build each meal around a protein food: eggs, chicken, goat, fish, soybeans, dal, curd or paneer.
  • Cooking oil is the hidden calorie. Each teaspoon adds about 40 calories, so measuring it is the easiest change most families can make.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in dal bhat?

A typical plate with 2 cups of rice, 1 cup of dal and 1 cup of saag has about 790 calories and 28 g of protein. Reducing the rice to 1 cup saves about 200 calories.

Which South Asian foods are highest in protein?

Per 100 g: chicken breast (about 31 g), goat meat (27 g), soybeans (18 g), eggs (12.5 g) and paneer (about 18 g on typical labels). Dal, rajma and chana have about 8–9 g per 100 g cooked.

Where do these numbers come from?

From USDA FoodData Central, the US government's food composition database. Each food page names the exact USDA entry and its ID. Where USDA has no entry, such as momo, we use the closest match and say so.

Why do my home-cooked dishes differ?

Recipes, oil and portion sizes vary. Cooking oil is the biggest variable: each teaspoon adds about 40 calories.
