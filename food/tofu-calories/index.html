Tofu Calories and Protein

1 piece (120 g) has about 73 calories and 8.6 g of protein. Soybean curd, bean curd.

73calories
8.6 gprotein
1.4 gcarbs
4.4 gfat

Tofu is made from soy milk pressed into blocks. Soft tofu like the one USDA measured has about 61 calories and 7 g of protein per 100 g; firm and extra-firm tofu have more of both because they contain less water.

To get 25 g of protein from tofu alone you would need about 349 g, which is 213 kcal.

Is tofu good for weight loss?

Excellent: very high protein for the calories. Use it in curries, bhurji or stir-fries instead of paneer to save fat.

Is it good for building muscle?

Useful plant protein, especially firm tofu. Check the label: firm tofu often has 12–17 g of protein per 100 g.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Press firm tofu for 15 minutes before cooking so it absorbs more spice.
  • Tofu bhurji is a quick vegan swap for egg bhurji.
  • Fried tofu absorbs oil and roughly triples in calories.
