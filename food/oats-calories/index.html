Oats Calories and Protein

40 g dry (a typical bowl) has about 152 calories and 5.3 g of protein. Rolled oats, quick oats, jai.

152calories
5.3 gprotein
27.1 gcarbs
2.6 gfat

Oats are whole grains rich in a soluble fibre called beta-glucan. A typical 40 g bowl (dry weight) has about 150 calories and 5 g of protein before milk or toppings.

To get 25 g of protein from oats alone you would need about 190 g, which is 721 kcal.

Is oats good for weight loss?

A very good breakfast: filling, high in fibre and easy to control. Watch sugar, honey and large spoonfuls of nut butter.

Is it good for building muscle?

Cooked in milk with a scoop of whey or two eggs on the side, oats make a strong high-protein breakfast.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Cook in milk instead of water for about 8 g more protein per cup of milk.
  • Add banana for sweetness instead of sugar.
  • Overnight oats with curd are a quick no-cook option.
