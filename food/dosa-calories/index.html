Dosa Calories and Protein

1 medium dosa (80 g) has about 167 calories and 4.6 g of protein. Plain dosa, sada dosa.

167calories
4.6 gprotein
29.7 gcarbs
3.2 gfat

Dosa is a thin, crisp South Indian crepe made from fermented rice and urad dal batter. A plain medium dosa has about 170 calories. Masala dosa adds a spiced potato filling and usually more oil.

Calories come from: Protein 11% · Carbs 72% · Fat 17%.

To get 25 g of protein from dosa alone you would need about 439 g, which is 917 kcal.

Is dosa good for weight loss?

A plain dosa with sambar is a reasonable meal. Watch the coconut chutney and the potato filling, which add calories quickly.

Is it good for building muscle?

Fermented rice and lentils give some protein, but not much. Add eggs or a side of sambar with extra dal.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Sambar adds protein and fibre for few calories.
  • Ask for less oil or ghee on the tawa.
  • Masala dosa can be 350–500 kcal at restaurants.
