Mutton Curry (Goat) Calories and Protein

1 cup with gravy (236 g) has about 222 calories and 10.6 g of protein. Khasi ko masu, goat curry, mutton masala.

222calories
10.6 gprotein
16.6 gcarbs
13.2 gfat

Mutton curry in Nepal and India is usually goat meat (khasi), cooked slowly with onion, tomato and spices. USDA's mixed lamb or mutton curry recipe averages 94 calories per 100 g with gravy. The meat itself is quite lean; most of the fat comes from oil and fatty cuts.

Is mutton curry good for weight loss?

Enjoy it in moderate portions with lots of vegetables. Lean pieces and less oil make a big difference.

Is it good for building muscle?

Goat meat is a rich protein source. Choose more meat and less gravy.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Goat meat is leaner than many cuts of lamb.
  • Bones and gravy look big but add little protein.
  • Festival mutton dishes are often cooked in more oil: just enjoy them.
