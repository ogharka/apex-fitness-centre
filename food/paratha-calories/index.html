Paratha Calories and Protein

1 paratha (79 g) has about 258 calories and 5 g of protein. Plain paratha, whole wheat paratha.

258calories
5 gprotein
35.8 gcarbs
10.4 gfat

Paratha is a layered whole wheat flatbread cooked with oil or ghee. The layers of fat make it richer than roti: about 260 kcal for one 79 g paratha, with about 10 g of fat. Stuffed parathas (aloo, gobi, paneer) are higher again.

To get 25 g of protein from paratha alone you would need about 393 g, which is 1,281 kcal.

Is paratha good for weight loss?

Best as an occasional breakfast. Use less ghee, pair it with curd and eggs rather than pickle and butter, and stop at one.

Is it good for building muscle?

A good calorie-dense option if you need to gain weight. Paneer or egg paratha adds useful protein.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Cooking with 1 tsp of oil instead of 1 tbsp saves about 80 kcal per paratha.
  • A bowl of curd with paratha adds protein and makes the meal more filling.
  • Aloo paratha adds potato carbs; paneer paratha adds protein.
