Roti (Chapati) Calories and Protein

1 medium roti (about 40 g) has about 119 calories and 4.5 g of protein. Chapati, phulka, sukkha roti.

119calories
4.5 gprotein
18.5 gcarbs
3 gfat

Roti is the everyday flatbread of Nepal, India and Pakistan, made from whole wheat flour (atta), water and sometimes a little oil. Home-made roti is usually smaller and thinner than the commercial roti USDA measured, so a typical home roti of about 40 g has around 120 calories.

To get 25 g of protein from roti alone you would need about 222 g, which is 660 kcal.

Is roti good for weight loss?

Good, in sensible portions. Two medium roti give about 240 kcal with some fibre. Ghee or butter on top adds about 45 kcal per teaspoon, so keep it dry or lightly brushed.

Is it good for building muscle?

Useful as an easy carb source around training. Pair it with a protein food (dal, eggs, chicken, paneer) because roti alone has only about 4–5 g of protein each.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Weigh one of your home roti once: size varies a lot from house to house.
  • Phulka (puffed, no oil) has fewer calories than roti cooked with oil or ghee.
  • Swap one roti for an extra cup of sabji or salad to cut about 120 kcal from a meal.
