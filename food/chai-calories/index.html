Milk Tea (Chai) Calories and Protein

1 cup (240 g) has about 118 calories and 3.7 g of protein. Chiya, doodh chiya, masala chai.

118calories
3.7 gprotein
21.6 gcarbs
2.2 gfat

Milk tea (chiya, chai) is drunk several times a day across South Asia. A cup has about 120 calories, but the real number depends on how much milk and sugar you add. Two teaspoons of sugar alone add about 30 calories.

Is milk tea good for weight loss?

The hidden calories are in the repeat cups. Four cups a day with sugar can be 400–500 kcal. Cut the sugar gradually or have fewer cups.

Is it good for building muscle?

Not a meaningful protein source. Fine to enjoy, but don't count on it for nutrition.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Halve the sugar for two weeks, then halve it again; taste adapts.
  • Black tea or green tea has almost no calories.
  • Avoid tea right after meals if you are low in iron; it can reduce iron absorption.
