Buffalo Milk Calories and Protein

1 glass (244 g / 1 cup) has about 237 calories and 9.2 g of protein. Bhaisi ko doodh, bhains ka doodh.

237calories

Buffalo milk is common in Nepal, India and Pakistan. It is creamier than cow's milk, with about twice the fat: roughly 235 calories per glass compared with about 150 for whole cow's milk.

Is buffalo milk good for weight loss?

Higher in calories, so use smaller amounts or dilute it, especially in several cups of tea a day.

Is it good for building muscle?

Great for people who struggle to gain weight: more calories per glass without more volume.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Skimming the cream (malai) after boiling lowers the fat.
  • Paneer and curd made from buffalo milk are richer too.
  • Four cups of milky tea a day with buffalo milk can add 200+ kcal.
