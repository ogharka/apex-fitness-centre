Boiled Egg Calories and Protein

1 large egg (50 g) has about 71 calories and 6.2 g of protein. Anda, phul, boiled or poached egg.

71calories
6.2 gprotein
0.4 gcarbs
4.7 gfat

Eggs are one of the cheapest high-quality proteins anywhere. One large boiled egg has about 71 calories and 6 g of protein. Fried eggs or omelettes add the calories of the oil or butter used.

To get 25 g of protein from a boiled egg alone you would need about 200 g, which is 285 kcal.

Is a boiled egg good for weight loss?

Excellent. Two or three eggs at breakfast keep many people full for hours.

Is it good for building muscle?

Excellent. Whole eggs provide complete protein; the yolk carries most of the vitamins.

Tips from the Apex trainers

  • Egg whites alone are about 17 kcal and 3.6 g protein each.
  • Boil a batch for the week to make breakfast and snacks easy.
  • Healthy adults can usually eat an egg or two a day; ask your doctor if you have high cholesterol.
